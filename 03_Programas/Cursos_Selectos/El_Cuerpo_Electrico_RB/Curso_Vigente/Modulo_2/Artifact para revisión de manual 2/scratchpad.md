# Deck: "El nodo de lesión" — Regulación Bioeléctrica

Audience: clinicians / trainees in the Programa El Cuerpo Eléctrico. Projection in a room.
Goal: NOT a text summary of the manual. A conceptual, visual narrative that makes the *why* and the *mechanism* land, with animated diagrams. ~10–12 min.

## Design system (reuse the manual's brand for cohesion)
- Fonts: SS4 (serif display), Plex (sans), PlexMono (data). From rb-fonts.css.
- Palette: ink #201F1C, paper #FCFBF7, teal #0F6E56, teal-deep #0B5644, teal-tint #E1F5EE, coral #D85A30, line #DEDBD2, faint #8B8A83.
- Two backgrounds only: paper (content) + deep-teal (section/impact). Parallel section headers.
- Type scale (1920×1080): title 76, huge-figure 150, subtitle 44, body 32, small 24, kicker 22 (tracked caps).
- Every conceptual slide carries ONE bespoke animated SVG/CSS diagram — geometric/diagrammatic, not illustrative.
